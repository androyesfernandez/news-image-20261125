One image that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026: 46 stories across 9 regions, 15 drawn as real vector scenes. The three hero leads are Shenzhou-24 lifting off at 00:18 UTC with Pakistan's first astronaut, Huawei convicted on 10 of 12 counts in Brooklyn, and the Houthis hitting Riyadh's PIF Tower again. Around them: Nvidia sliding 5.9% after its one-day rally, Dell's record $54.9bn AI quarter, the Solomon Islands treaty signed in Canberra, the Gaza security chief killed, the CIA breach, 20 hostages in Cartagena, the Thanksgiving storm, the EU–Western Balkans summit, Hera's first close-ups of Dimorphos, the Kospi opening −3.9%, and the Senate passing Australia's Digital Duty of Care.

Every story in the poster happened, or was first reported, on 25 November 2026 in some time zone — Tuesday-evening US news counts, because it was already Wednesday in Sydney and Tokyo. The red DATE CHECK box names the near-misses that were left out on purpose: the 700,000 Russian death toll (23 Nov), the NATO space-defence vow (23 Nov), the Seoul education minister's resignation (22 Nov), the Xiamen jet order (24 Nov), the North Korea Swiss-office threat (24 Nov), the Cygnus and USSF-380 launches (29 Nov), the Poland missile landings (20 Nov), and the ICJ Myanmar ruling (19 Nov).

The poster is a cream broadsheet in serif throughout: masthead and giant red date, a region strip with counts computed from the story bank, a UTC timeline, a six-numbers row (each number tied to a drawn story), a dotted world map with numbered pins, 31 more stories in brief, and a sources footer.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only). The program copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, 377,096 bytes) to `/output/entry.png`.
